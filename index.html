Our story

We didn’t learn these trails from a map.

Himalaya Ascent began with a handful of guides raised in the valleys they now walk with you. The belief that started it is simple: the mountains deserve to be met slowly, safely, and with someone who reads both the weather and the people.

We stay small on purpose. Every itinerary is shaped by hand, paced around real acclimatization, and backed by First-Aid-trained leads and round-the-clock support.
